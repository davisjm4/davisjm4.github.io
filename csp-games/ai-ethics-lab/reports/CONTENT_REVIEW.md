# Content review and scope

Author review: 2026-10-25. External educator/classroom review: pending.

The 100 frames are 20 different ethical/evidence focuses applied through five distinct reasoning tasks: identify a supported concern, select a bounded response, evaluate a tradeoff, predict a supported risk, or evaluate a claim. Across them there are 25 concept labels and 20 application domains. A changed organization name is not counted as a family.

Each frame specifies the evidence or immediate goal that supports its key. The table below records the scope and author rationale for every family. cases.js retains all alternatives and scenario facts. review-fixtures.js independently pins 16 high-risk distinctions to a specified key and rationale. These are author-reviewed fixtures, not a second human or educator certification.

Review corrections before release:
- Replaced unrelated consequence distractors with same-context misconceptions and unsupported leaps.
- Replaced branding, interface and word-count fillers in response/evidence questions with relevant but insufficient actions or measurements.
- Evidence variants no longer append a fact that already supplies the requested measurement. Their five variants change review scope, independent checking, version carryover, audience or earlier-review relevance.
- Error details distinguish relevant error types without substituting a false-rejection statistic for a false-alarm scenario.
- Fairness details do not silently choose the unresolved error-cost priority.
- Keys and feedback distinguish security from appropriate purpose, disclosure from usable choice, notice from case explanation, task savings from job outcomes, per-unit cost from total/net effects, realism from authorization, correlation from causation and attribution from permission.

Medical, hiring, lending and public-service cases are fictional and bounded. They do not prescribe clinical thresholds, real eligibility decisions, jurisdiction-specific legal outcomes or one universal fairness measure. Permission cases state an explicit project policy rather than asserting unsettled AI copyright rules. No partisan platform or personal profile is used. Human oversight, extra data, transparency and a single metric are never treated as automatic guarantees.

Composition is finite: five evidence/operating details per family, with three distractor-set combinations. Some operational details are shared by related families. The same conceptual misconception can recur even without an exact repeat. Narrow mode/topic pools have only 20 instances; this limitation is visible.

Automated tests reject malformed instances and check known misconception fixtures. They cannot prove that all ethical decisions have a uniquely acceptable answer. No ambiguity rejections are attributed to a machine moral oracle. Author review admitted 500 scoped instances; external classroom review should assess reading load, alternative interpretations, distractor plausibility, cueing and examples across local communities.

Answer-length audit across the 500 instances: 56 keys are longest (11.2%), 171 shortest (34.2%), including ties. The key is not consistently longest; length neutrality is not perfect. This descriptive heuristic is not a claim that wording provides zero clues. Random answer positions and scenario reasoning remain important.
