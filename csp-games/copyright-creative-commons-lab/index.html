License reference and practical distinctions

These original fictional cases focus on U.S. copyright and Creative Commons 4.0 unless a case says otherwise. Educational guidance, not legal advice. A license grants permission subject to conditions. Fair use is a separate legal exception. Missing facts can prevent a definite answer.

BY · Attribution
Give appropriate credit, link the license, retain supplied required information and indicate changes when sharing modified material.
SA · ShareAlike
When sharing an adaptation, use the same or an applicable compatible license. It does not force publication of a private draft or automatically relicense separate works in a collection.
NC · NonCommercial
The use must not be primarily directed toward commercial advantage or monetary compensation. Being a school or nonprofit alone does not settle this.
ND · NoDerivatives
The license does not authorize sharing adapted material. Version 4.0 can permit making private adaptations; NC still applies where present. Purely technical format changes are different.
CC0 · Public-domain dedication
The rights holder attempts to waive copyright and related rights to the extent legally possible. It is separate from the six licenses and does not clear every privacy, publicity or trademark issue.

Six licenses: BY · BY-SA · BY-ND · BY-NC · BY-NC-SA · BY-NC-ND. All require attribution; combine the other conditions as named.

TASL: Title when supplied, Author, Source and License is a useful credit framework, not a rigid universal legal formula. Include license links, required supplied notices and modification indications; do not invent unavailable information. Earlier license versions can differ.

Public domain: Generally no copyright permission is needed for reuse of a verified public-domain work. Free-to-view, old-looking or unknown-author content is not automatically public domain. New additions and other people’s rights may remain relevant.

Fair use: Consider purpose and character, nature of the work, amount and substantiality, and potential market effect together. School use, credit, no profit, a percentage or a number of seconds never guarantees the outcome. Criticism, parody or a new purpose can matter without settling the case.
