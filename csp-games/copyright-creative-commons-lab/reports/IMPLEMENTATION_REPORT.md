## Added activity

One primary format: **Choose the Best Action**. Mixed is the default; the only filter is topic. Each round presents one short fictional situation, one decision and four choices. A first click scores exactly once and locks all choices. Feedback identifies correct/incorrect, the best-supported answer, a reason specific to the selected misconception, the governing rule and a concise clarification. Primary-source links appear after submission. Next, Help and filter changes are unscored.

Each family has one authored, validated case. Cosmetic substitutions and answer permutations are not counted as new families. This bounded bank meets a normal 50-round Mixed session without exact repeats. History is local and shared across topic changes and reloads within the daily session. The previous three families are avoided when eligible. A topic has only 10 families: when exhausted, its least-recent eligible case repeats. The interface explains that limit rather than making a false 50-round guarantee for a small topic.

Mixed sampling balances topic exposure, retains a 50-case recent history and shuffles all choices with a reproducible seeded generator. Foundational/applied cases predominate; there are no separate difficulty modes. Correct choices are not consistently longest; a test bounds that cue to no more than half the bank.

## Accuracy and content boundaries

All six CC 4.0 licenses and CC0 are represented. Reviewed primary sources are linked in [CONTENT_REVIEW.md](CONTENT_REVIEW.md), which includes every scenario, best answer, rule, clarification and distractor rationale. The authored review and separate fixture file bind all 60 decisions to their governing facts; an independent license-policy table exercises the six combinations and counterfactual fact changes. Tests do not substitute for the source review or professional legal judgment.

The lab distinguishes license permission, license conditions, legal exceptions and insufficient facts. ND 4.0 allows producing private adaptations while restricting sharing; necessary format conversion alone is not adaptation. SA does not force publication or automatically relicense separate collection items. NC depends on the use, not simply school/nonprofit status. CC0 does not clear all third-party rights. Attribution is practical TASL guidance plus required supplied information, reasonable context, license links and modification indications; one expressly labeled CC BY 3.0 case demonstrates a version difference.

Fair-use cases ask for a relevant consideration, rejection of a false automatic rule or acknowledgment that facts are insufficient. They never produce a numeric permission score or a definitive verdict from one factor. Criticism, parody, educational and commercial contexts, nature, unpublished works, qualitative amount and market substitution are included. No copyrighted media is reproduced.
