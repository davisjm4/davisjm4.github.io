How to play

1Click a link, or focus it and press Enter or Space, to remove it.
2Use the fewest links possible to disconnect Target A from Target B.
3Check your answer. Undo or clear links any time before checking.

Example: If two routes connect A to B, breaking one link may leave the other route open. Your cut must block every route.

The links you choose form a minimum cut. The network starts connected, with multiple routes that provide redundancy.

What you’re practicing

Redundancy lets information travel along alternate routes. Fault tolerance is a network’s ability to keep working when a link fails. A minimum cut is the smallest set of links whose removal disconnects two targets.
