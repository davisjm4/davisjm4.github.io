# Known limitations — VEX Component Challenge

## Technical and instructional boundaries

- This is decision practice with stated assumptions, not a full mechanical assembly or physics simulator. It does not calculate structural load capacity, drivetrain dynamics, manufacturing tolerance, friction, wear or safe operating limits.
- The same general hardware function can support several real solutions. A scored scenario is only as specific as its provided dimensions, interfaces and constraints. Apply its reasoning to the stated case rather than treating the answer as a universal rule.
- Existing catalog photographs are not calibrated drawings. Appearance alone does not establish thread, material, shaft family, hole alignment or dimensional fit. Scenario constraints and actual measurement must supply those facts.
- Official sources support the cited hardware facts. Original scenarios are AI-assisted authored exercises; they are not official VEX questions or human-certified engineering advice. Independent VEX educator review has not been obtained.
- Broad links to V5 STEM Lab concepts identify supplemental classroom use. They do not establish formal standards alignment or official endorsement for every individual question.

## Counts and repeat control

Use the measured family and instance totals in QUESTION_GENERATOR_VALIDATION.md. The requested 100-family/300-instance figures are targets, not automatically delivered counts. Choice order, synonyms and irrelevant cosmetic changes must not be counted as new reasoning families or new technical cases.

Narrow mode/difficulty/category combinations can contain too few cases to maintain a 50-round exact-instance cooldown. Such repetitions must be disclosed in the interface and audit rather than hidden. Avoiding an immediate family repeat is only possible when the eligible pool contains another family. A filter with no eligible case should explain that condition instead of silently changing its constraints.

## Device, accessibility and physical transfer

Only the tests and browser checks actually recorded in the release evidence are verified. Responsive browser widths are not physical Chromebook or phone tests; pointer clicks are not proof of touchscreen operation; accessible labels and keyboard checks are not a substitute for real screen-reader speech testing. Treat any unrecorded physical-device, touch or native screen-reader workflow as pending.

Correct answers and first-try accuracy demonstrate decisions within supplied scenarios. Physical part retrieval, safe tool handling, correct dimensions, actual assembly quality and fault diagnosis require separate observation. The game does not replace hands-on STEM Labs, physical testing or engineering notebook evidence.

## Local data and assets

Practice history and session reporting are local to the browser/device. Cleared storage, private browsing restrictions or a different device can remove or separate progress. The activity should remain usable in memory when persistent storage is blocked, with an explicit notice. No student account, public leaderboard or cross-device synchronization is included.

Images reused from Parts Sprint remain subject to their original VEX educational-use and attribution conditions. The reuse does not create an unrestricted commercial license. No new manufacturer endorsement or commercial permission is implied.

## Scope

Build 3 does not include programming, VEXcode, virtual driving, multiplayer, teacher reporting, Canvas QTI, student accounts, Tinkercad or VEX VR integration, or expansion of Parts Sprint/Course Architect. No future feature is claimed as implemented.
