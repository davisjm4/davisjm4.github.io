Think before you build

  1. Identify the required mechanical function.
  2. Check the stated dimensions and interfaces.
  3. Choose hardware that meets every constraint.
  4. Explain why the alternatives do not fit.

These are constrained planning examples. A correct answer does not certify physical assembly or safe operation. Follow the actual build instructions and test the mechanism.
