How scoring and images work

Correct first attempts earn 1 progress unit. A streak of at least 3 adds 0.1. A corrected or reference-assisted answer earns 0.4. Personal bests compare accuracy first, then first-try correct answers, longest streak, and response time. At least 5 independent attempts are required; ended-early races do not set bests. Faster guessing cannot improve first-try accuracy.

Descriptions support nonvisual practice. Tool-size and shaft-size questions include reference measurements or markings; photos are not all the same scale. This catalog covers V5-compatible classroom, competition and Workcell parts, not the contents of every kit. Image provenance and conditions.
