# Mastery and evidence

## CSG-MAS-01 — One evidence model

**Approved · implemented for supported sources. Roles:** learners, teachers, curriculum authors. Record distinct scopes: daily activity metrics, long-term independent practice, synced class practice, formative proficiency, published practice requirements, teacher assessment score and verified mastery. Eligibility depends on the scored question's actual assessed mode and family, including questions drawn by Mixed Practice. Game difficulty describes the challenge. Evidence quality determines whether a question supports a formative tier. A single-difficulty activity may support multiple tiers if audited evidence warrants it.

**States/data:** unmapped, ineligible, eligible, duplicate, provisional, verified and retired-policy evidence. Keep stable source attempt, question and session IDs, timestamp, activity, assessed mode, family, credited skill, first-attempt flag, correctness and permitted difficulty/subskill metadata. Keep original records immutable and idempotent across retries and replays. First-try credit requires a supported, independent first submission. Skips, reveal, retry, elapsed time, visiting a page or a familiarity label produce no first-try mastery credit. A retry may appear in daily reporting under its own meaning. Server adapters own enrollment validation and trust. **Dependencies:** CSG-CUR-02, CSG-SEC-01/02. **Accessibility:** explain source, time and denominator in plain language. **Acceptance:** focus and Mixed credit the actual assessed skill once. Duplicate sync, assist, retry and unsupported formats cannot inflate first attempts or verified evidence.

## CSG-MAS-02 — Formative level and official assessment

**Approved · official scoring and formative tiers implemented, independent presentation and evaluation pending. Roles:** learners, authorized teachers. Show official teacher-reviewed score or verified mastery above formative evidence. Level 3 is sufficient for progression and Level 4 is optional. Recognize appropriate teacher-reviewed evidence through performance tasks, project or portfolio, explanation/interview, or objective assessment. Offer a demonstration route where automatically assessed Level 4 is unavailable. Preserve the difference between numeric 0–4 score, a policy-bound mastery award, class goal completion, and a computed formative level. Use no-score and insufficient-evidence states separately from a recorded zero. Show the meaning of the evidence window when cumulative results and tier results differ.

**Baseline:** the [formative evaluator](../cs-games/pathway/proficiency/index.mjs) uses audited tiers. Current automatic Level 4 requires advanced and intermediate tier criteria. Level 3 can be shown with no beginner set. Advancement currently checks formative Level 3+ or a teacher score of at least 3. Existing practice-only and provisional semantics remain. This release preserves the course rubric and tier eligibility. Higher-tier substitution or new automatic evidence requires a separate product decision. **Data/security:** only authorized review records or adjusts official outcomes. Retain assessment history, evidence type and policy/version context. **Dependencies:** CSG-TCH-03, CSG-CUR-02, CSG-SEC-01. **Acceptance:** paired synthetic states show a meaningful Level 3 and a separate teacher 4/4. Level 4 remains optional. Unsupported questions cannot be promoted by title, difficulty or activity label.

## CSG-MAS-03 — Versioned requirements and pacing

**Approved · versioned publishing exists, evaluator separation pending (CSG-DEC-09). Roles:** teacher, enrolled learner. Label three independent results in skill views and reports. Use the same terminology in preview and published views.

| Result | Owner and input | Effect of publishing a practice target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Official teacher-reviewed mastery | Authorized teacher assessment, with evidence and recorded policy context. Display first where present. | Recorded scores, verified outcomes and assessment history are preserved. Only the authorized assessment workflow changes a score. |
| Formative course proficiency | Course rubric and eligible ordinary practice within the learner’s current scope. | With identical evidence and course rubric, the level and course progression remain the same. A new target, stricter or looser, can neither lower nor raise the formative level. |
| Class practice target progress | Published class, area or skill rule, with its version, effective window and eligible counts. | The current target and its status may change. Keep prior evaluations, evidence and policy versions with their original meaning. |

**States/data:** local edit, saved draft, preview, published, conflict and rollback as a new revision. Keep historical policy and curriculum snapshots and original attempts with provenance. Show whether the displayed result is current, historical or unavailable. Preserve an existing policy-bound practice award as a historical result with its revision, distinct from a teacher-reviewed score. **Security:** only an authorized teacher can publish, and historical context remains class-scoped. **Dependencies:** CSG-TCH-04, CSG-SEC-01, CSG-PKG-08. **Accessibility:** give all three results text labels, including revision or date where it explains a change. **Acceptance:** CSG-AT-05 and CSG-AT-11 through 13.

**Approved migration policy (CSG-DEC-14):** preserve historically earned progression even if an independent evaluation yields a different level. Keep historical proficiency with its original policy context and present accurate current formative proficiency separately. Explain differences and carried-forward progression to both teachers and students. Keep original evidence intact and teacher-verified scores unchanged. Require side-by-side replay and regression tests before deploying evaluator changes.

### Current behavior and proposed migration

**Current baseline:** the [shared formative evaluator](../cs-games/pathway/proficiency/index.mjs) accepts a configured teacher policy that can override tier minimum question count, accuracy and minimum correct. Policy presence also affects whether some supplemental nodes count toward area progression. The [student pathway service](../wpr-games/workplace-readiness/service/pathways.mjs) supplies published policies to the evaluator, and the baseline [test](../cs-games/pathway/tests/proficiency.test.mjs) expects a Level 3 fixture to evaluate at Level 1 with a stricter policy. The audit observed the drop in a synthetic class. The account-free guest path uses course evidence without a class policy. This coupling is a known gap against the approved behavior.

1. **Inventory and characterize.** Trace Worker inputs, practice-target attempt windows and restarts, caches, area readiness, recommendations, student views, teacher reports and exports. The current status-history write records pathway status and a checkpoint digest, so it cannot establish by itself that historical per-skill formative levels were stored. Determine which historical results were persisted and which can be reproduced from retained evidence and policy versions. Mark incomplete provenance unavailable. A historical coupled result must retain its original context.
2. **Run side-by-side in isolation.** On the same scoped attempt records, compare the legacy result, an independent course-rubric result and the existing versioned class-target result. Preserve the normal evidence eligibility and deduplication rules. Verify that changing or restarting a target window cannot change which evidence the course evaluator receives. Measure both directions of cutover discrepancy and effects on progression, reporting and recommendations. Use synthetic fixtures representing older revisions and incomplete history.
3. **Cut over compatibly.** Proposed mechanism: add clearly labeled result fields and version compatible API, cache and export readers before retiring the coupled field. Keep old results with provenance where available and leave source evidence and assessments intact. Verify carried-forward progression and separate historical and current labels against CSG-DEC-14 before cutover. Use additive migration and rehearse rollback with compatible readers that preserve writes and the approved separation.
4. **Gate rollout.** Require CSG-AT-05 and CSG-AT-11 through 13, compare both user roles and legacy exports, and verify no writes to evidence or official scores from target publication, migration or rollback. The documentation update authorizes no cutover.

## CSG-MAS-04 — Assigned practice uses ordinary evidence

**Approved · assignment path planned. Roles:** learners and teachers. Issuing, editing, switching, completing, restricting, releasing, ending or reassigning work leaves proficiency and mastery records intact. A qualifying attempt in either assignment scope enters the ordinary first-attempt and proficiency evaluator exactly as the same activity would outside an assignment. Assignment completion is a separate progress fact. Reusing one attempt for assignment status never creates another evidence event. Linking an assignment to an activity does not certify an unsupported mode or change the normal evidence path. Verified mastery continues through CSG-MAS-02 and CSG-TCH-03.

**States/data:** pending sync, eligible, duplicate, practice-only and teacher-reviewed. Store optional assignment references separately from evidence identity and eligibility. **Dependencies:** CSG-TDL-01/02/04, CSG-MAS-01/02. **Accessibility:** label practice credit and assignment completion distinctly. **Security:** same authorized profile and scoped event pipeline. Guest or unverifiable activity events cannot fulfill class assignments or be promoted into enrolled evidence. **Acceptance:** CSG-AT-TDL-06 and 07 compare assigned and ordinary practice and preserve prior awards through all assignment lifecycle transitions.
