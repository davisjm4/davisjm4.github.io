# Mastery and evidence

## CSG-MAS-01 — One evidence model

**Approved · implemented for supported sources. Roles:** learners, teachers, curriculum authors. Record distinct scopes: daily activity metrics, long-term independent practice, synced class practice, formative proficiency, published practice requirements, teacher assessment score and verified mastery. Eligibility depends on the scored question's actual assessed mode and family, including questions drawn by Mixed Practice. Game difficulty describes the challenge. Evidence quality determines whether a question supports a formative tier. A single-difficulty activity may support multiple tiers if audited evidence warrants it.

**States/data:** unmapped, ineligible, eligible, duplicate, provisional, verified and retired-policy evidence. Keep stable source attempt, question and session IDs, timestamp, activity, assessed mode, family, credited skill, first-attempt flag, correctness and permitted difficulty/subskill metadata. Keep original records immutable and idempotent across retries and replays. First-try credit requires a supported, independent first submission. Skips, reveal, retry, elapsed time, visiting a page or a familiarity label produce no first-try mastery credit. A retry may appear in daily reporting under its own meaning. Server adapters own enrollment validation and trust. **Dependencies:** CSG-CUR-02, CSG-SEC-01/02. **Accessibility:** explain source, time and denominator in plain language. **Acceptance:** focus and Mixed credit the actual assessed skill once. Duplicate sync, assist, retry and unsupported formats cannot inflate first attempts or verified evidence.

## CSG-MAS-02 — Formative level and official assessment

**Approved · implemented, hierarchy needs repair. Roles:** learners, authorized teachers. Show official teacher-reviewed score or verified mastery above formative evidence. Level 3 is sufficient for progression and Level 4 is optional. Recognize appropriate teacher-reviewed evidence through performance tasks, project or portfolio, explanation/interview, or objective assessment. Offer a demonstration route where automatically assessed Level 4 is unavailable. Preserve the difference between numeric 0–4 score, a policy-bound mastery award, class goal completion, and a computed formative level. Use no-score and insufficient-evidence states separately from a recorded zero. Show the meaning of the evidence window when cumulative results and tier results differ.

**Baseline:** the [formative evaluator](../cs-games/pathway/proficiency/index.mjs) uses audited tiers. Current automatic Level 4 requires advanced and intermediate tier criteria. Level 3 can be shown with no beginner set. Advancement currently checks formative Level 3+ or a teacher score of at least 3. Existing practice-only and provisional semantics remain. Higher-tier substitution, retrospective reclassification, and unsupported automatic evidence remain review decisions. **Data/security:** only authorized review records or adjusts official outcomes. Retain assessment history, evidence type and policy/version context. **Dependencies:** CSG-TCH-03, CSG-CUR-02, CSG-SEC-01. **Acceptance:** paired synthetic states show a meaningful Level 3 and a separate teacher 4/4. Level 4 remains optional. Unsupported questions cannot be promoted by title, difficulty or activity label.

## CSG-MAS-03 — Versioned requirements and pacing

**Approved · implemented. Roles:** teacher, enrolled learner. Version class defaults, area overrides and skill-specific requirements. Use draft, preview, publish, conflict and rollback-as-new-revision. Persist attempt history and evidence; policy revisions may produce a new evaluation window. Distinguish current results and historical awards. A published requirement does not set, revoke or erase a teacher's 0–4 assessment. Explain changed policy context to a learner before interpreting a changed result.

**Baseline and open decision:** current configured teacher policy can supply minimum count, accuracy and minimum-correct thresholds to the formative tier evaluator. Claude observed a Level 3 to Level 1 change after publication in a synthetic profile. Existing behavior is preserved until CSG-OPEN-08 is approved. Recommended default for review: show published practice target progress independently from course-calibrated formative proficiency, after compatibility and impact review. Verify same-profile, same-evidence policy changes, restarts, concurrent publication and replay. **Data/security:** owner authorization, immutable published policy and curriculum snapshots, versioned attempt windows. **Dependencies:** CSG-TCH-04, CSG-SEC-01. **Accessibility:** present draft/published/changed state in text and expose policy meaning at both ends. **Acceptance:** a changed rule reaches the student only after publish, with previous evidence and official score preserved. Any resulting formative change is labeled and explained.

## CSG-MAS-04 — Assigned practice uses ordinary evidence

**Approved · assignment path planned. Roles:** learners and teachers. Issuing, editing, switching, completing, restricting, releasing, ending or reassigning work leaves proficiency and mastery records intact. A qualifying attempt in either assignment scope enters the ordinary first-attempt and proficiency evaluator exactly as the same activity would outside an assignment. Assignment completion is a separate progress fact. Reusing one attempt for assignment status never creates another evidence event. Linking an assignment to an activity does not certify an unsupported mode or change the normal evidence path. Verified mastery continues through CSG-MAS-02 and CSG-TCH-03.

**States/data:** pending sync, eligible, duplicate, practice-only and teacher-reviewed. Store optional assignment references separately from evidence identity and eligibility. **Dependencies:** CSG-TDL-01/02/04, CSG-MAS-01/02. **Accessibility:** label practice credit and assignment completion distinctly. **Security:** same authorized profile and scoped event pipeline. **Acceptance:** CSG-AT-TDL-06 and 07 compare assigned and ordinary practice and preserve prior awards through all assignment lifecycle transitions.
