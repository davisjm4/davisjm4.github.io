# Student experience

## CSG-STU-01 — Independent start and persistence

**Approved · implemented in part. Roles:** guests, enrolled students using independent practice. Start from course selection, open the full course map, search and explore any practice-ready skill. Make the scope clear before launch, including limits on tracking. Present one contextual recommendation with a plain reason. Keep progress and backup accessible in My Session or Independent History. Self-reported familiarity grants no proficiency, unlock, mastery, or evidence. Remove proficiency-like familiarity controls from primary skill actions. Optional private notes or pins require approval.

**States/data:** new, resumed, paused saving, storage unavailable, backed up, restored, practice-only. Guest history stays in the browser. Imported backups use validation and dedupe and remain unverified. Enrolled browsers can explicitly use guest practice with separate attribution. **Dependencies:** CSG-NAV-01/02, CSG-SES-02, CSG-SEC-02. **Accessibility:** no account modal blocks a guest, recovery copy and actions have accessible labels. **Acceptance:** start, practice, return and reload without an account. An eligible mapped answer appears once. An unsupported activity communicates its scope at launch and result. Familiarity or backup cannot create verified records.

## CSG-STU-02 — Join, restore and enter class

**Approved · implemented in part. Roles:** enrolled student. A class invitation shows class context, Join, and how to keep the recovery credential. Existing profiles restore with a pseudonymous ID and secret. Show enrollment and restoration as distinct tasks. After sign-in, provide My Class with assigned work, class progress, resume, and account actions. Pacing targets clearly distinguish suggested dates from assignments or deadlines. **States/data:** invitation valid/expired, join success, credential saved, restoring, invalid, revoked, offline and no class. Authenticated progress syncs by authorized profile. Keep recovery out of logs and reports. **Dependencies:** CSG-SEC-01, CSG-NAV-01. **Accessibility:** labeled inputs and status, focus on actionable errors, paste allowed. **Acceptance:** enroll in each registered course with a synthetic identity, restore on another browser, and observe only that profile's data. Invalid links and expired sessions offer safe recovery.

## CSG-STU-03 — Skill details and progression

**Approved direction · partial. Roles:** guest and enrolled. Prioritize the skill name and official teacher-reviewed result if present, next meaningful action, then provisional practice and requirements details. Display course formative proficiency, current class practice target progress and official teacher-reviewed mastery as independently labeled results. A target revision can change target status while course proficiency and the recorded score remain the same for unchanged evidence and rubric. Show the target revision or window. During evaluator transition, distinguish current formative proficiency from historical results and explain any difference and carried-forward progression (CSG-DEC-14). Explain the eligible evidence window and distinguish missing evidence, low accuracy, requirement met and verified mastery. Level 3 is sufficient to progress, and Level 4 is optional. Practice-only activities or unsupported levels explain available evidence routes. Keep Map/List and a clear Back to Course Map. **States/data:** not started, practice only, eligible evidence, goal met, verified result, review due and no coverage. Course and skill selection restore independently of scores. **Dependencies:** CSG-MAS-01/02/03, CSG-UX-02, CSG-NAV-03. **Accessibility:** mobile detail sheet or page remains dismissible and focuses the selected skill. **Acceptance:** a teacher-reviewed 4/4 appears above a lower formative result with both interpretable. Students can move on at Level 3 and access optional Level 4 without implying an official score.

## CSG-STU-04 — Assigned-work presentation

**Approved · planned. Roles:** enrolled. Follow CSG-TDL-02/04. **Today's Classwork** precedes **Your Catch-Up Work**. Students may switch between them, change modes, or practice another activity at any time. An accessible, dismissible reminder keeps unfinished work visible. Preserve practice progress and supported same-tab question state. Offer Resume Previous Path and Continue From Here when applicable.

**States/data:** available, started, complete, excused, cached or awaiting sync. Retain the selected pathway position independently of assignment progress. Continue ordinary locally available practice offline. Guest attempts stay in the separate independent scope. **Dependencies:** CSG-TDL-01–04, CSG-MAS-04, CSG-UX-03. **Accessibility:** clear section labels, keyboard Start/Resume, polite reminders and no automatic focus change. **Acceptance:** CSG-AT-TDL-01–04 and 10.

CSG-NAV-04 clarifies primary navigation: activity nodes launch practice directly when their skills use the same game. Individual skill details and demonstration planning remain available within practice and through legacy/detail links. The parent activity aggregates required skill targets, preserves optional Level 4 access, and recommends the next incomplete required activity. Returning restores the originating map instead of requiring another skill-map traversal.
