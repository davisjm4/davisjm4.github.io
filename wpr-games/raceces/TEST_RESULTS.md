### What the generation sweep covered

32094 generated challenges across all three modes and difficulties. Each one was validated before display (source passage, writing question, two distinct evidence statements, matched explanations, no placeholders, one gap and four options for Missing Piece, an unsolved start for Rearrange), graded with its correct answer, and graded again with every wrong answer. Rearrange additionally accepts both evidence-pair orders; a separate exhaustive test grades all 5,040 orderings of each of the 234 writing models against an independent oracle and requires exactly two accepted orders.
