Workplace Readiness Games is an independent practice resource, aligned to the publicly published Workplace Readiness Skills framework of the Career and Technical Consortium of States (CTECS). It is not an official CTECS product, it does not use official or retired test questions, and it does not guarantee results on any assessment. For official practice materials, visit wrs.ctecs.org/practice.
